# Fair Value / Price Ranking, October 2026

Cross-report review of the 32 saved `runs/*/report.md` files. Each report's headline probability-weighted fair value and its quoted price give the weighted ratio. The modal ratio uses the fair value of the highest-probability outcome. Modal ties are broken by the higher outcome value.

## Basis

- Values are the whole-dollar figures published in each report. Reports with prices near $1–$15 are imprecise. OPEN's unrounded weighted value is $1.17, a ratio of about 0.59, which would move it above XOM. QXO's unrounded quote is slightly above its weighted value, so its ratio is just under 1.00.
- Sensitivities (review-adjusted, rate-shifted) are not used.
- Quotes are October 14, 2026 closes except ADBE, ALB, BABA, BE, DUOL, FISV, INTU, KLAR, LULU, NOW, SAP and WMT, which use October 13 closes.
- Quotes are secondary-source and uncorroborated in most reports. INTU's $399 is described by its report as mechanically verified but not fully corrected.
- NTLA's two top outcomes are tied at 30%. The $37 outcome is used; the $2 outcome would give a modal ratio of 0.18.

## Review Notes

- A ratio compares a scenario value with a quote; it is not a prediction or a margin of safety. The reports state that quotes never shaped outcomes or probabilities.
- A ratio above 1.00 says the report's value exceeds the quote, not that the report is right. Verify the highest-ratio reports first (CHTR, FISV, ADBE, SFM).
- In reports whose quote exceeds every modeled outcome (for example AVGO, META, CAT, GOOGL, WMT, TSLA, RKLB, MRNA), the gap may partly reflect conventions such as the 9.39% hurdle and constant share count.
